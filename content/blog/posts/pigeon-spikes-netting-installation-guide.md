**Quick answer:** Use spikes on ledges, parapets and beams. Use net for open spaces such as balconies and shafts. Fix the net taut on a wire or rope frame with no gaps.

Pigeons are a nuisance on ledges, ducts and balconies in Indore and nearby areas. **Pigeon spikes** and **pigeon netting** are two common solutions. This guide explains use cases, installation basics, and where to buy in Siyaganj so you can choose the right option.

## Pigeon Spikes (Bird Spikes)

Pigeon spikes are strips of plastic or stainless steel with upward-pointing spikes. They are fixed on ledges, parapets, sign boards, and beams so birds cannot land. They are designed to discourage perching, not to injure birds.

- **Plastic spikes:** UV-stabilised; economical; suitable for most ledges.
- **SS (stainless steel) spikes:** Stronger and more durable; used where appearance or long life matters.

[Pigeon Spikes](/products/pigeon-spikes) are available in plastic and SS at our Siyaganj counter.

## Pigeon Netting

Pigeon netting is a mesh (nylon or HDPE) that blocks birds from entering balconies, ducts, windows, and shafts. It is fixed with hooks or frames and is almost invisible from a distance.

- **Use:** Balconies, windows, duct openings, and open shafts.
- **Material:** Nylon or HDPE; various colours and mesh sizes.

[Pigeon Net](/products/pigeon-nets) is available in different sizes; custom cut to fit is common.

## Use Cases in Indore

In Indore, spikes and netting are used on residential and commercial buildings:

- **Ledges and parapets:** Spikes are ideal. Fix along the length of the ledge.
- **Balconies and ducts:** Netting is better to block entry while allowing airflow.
- **Sign boards and canopies:** Spikes on top prevent birds from sitting.
- **Terraces:** Combination of spikes on parapet and netting for open areas if needed.

Projects in Dewas, Pithampur, and Ujjain also use spikes and netting for similar applications.

## Installation Basics

**Spikes:**  
- Clean the surface and fix the strip the way the spike maker specifies for that surface (adhesive, screws or clips). Cover the whole ledge so there is no gap to land in.

**Netting:**  
- Measure the opening. Fix hooks or a frame at the perimeter with anchors suited to the wall and the net maker's instructions. Stretch and attach the net, keeping the tension even.

## FAQs

**Which is better, spikes or netting?**  
Spikes suit ledges and flat surfaces where you want to prevent perching. Netting suits openings like balconies and ducts where you need to block entry.

**Do spikes harm birds?**  
They are designed to make landing uncomfortable, not to injure. A damaged or badly fitted strip can still hurt birds, so fit it as the maker directs and check it.

**Can I install pigeon spikes myself?**  
On a low, easy-to-reach ledge, yes, using the maker's fixing method. For high or large areas, use a professional installer.
