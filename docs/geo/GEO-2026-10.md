# GEO / search foundations, October 2026

Scope: make hmibrahim.net accurate, crawlable and quotable for Google, Bing and AI answer engines, in English and
Hindi, and measure real enquiries. No ranking or citation is promised. Branch: `geo-foundations` (built on `product-reviews-rail-faq`). Nothing is deployed.

## 3. Implemented

| Change | Why it matters commercially | Files |
|---|---|---|
| Instagram added to Organization and HardwareStore `sameAs` | Ties the shop's strongest social proof (reels, 2.8k followers) to the same entity as the site and Google listing | `content/site.ts` |
| `inLanguage` removed from Product | Keeps product markup valid | product `page.tsx` |
| Hindi TMT weight chart and wire gauge chart, with hreflang pairs and sitemap entries | Hindi buyers ask these exact questions; both are the pages most likely to be quoted | `content/blog/posts-hi/*` |
| TMT guide: rounding note (0.888 vs 0.889) and 40 ft bar weight | Answers the variants people actually search; `updated` bumped to 2026-10-12 | `content/blog/posts*/tmt-bar-weight-chart-size-guide-indore.md` |
| Crawler policy in one file; training bots separable from search and answer bots | One switch if the owner wants to opt out of model training without affecting search | `lib/crawlerPolicy.ts`, `app/robots.ts` |
| `llms.txt`: Instagram, language note, Hindi guides | Same facts as the site, one more entry point; it is a convenience file, not a ranking factor | `public/llms.txt` |
| Review rail under product photos (up to 10 verbatim Google reviews, recency-ranked) and a centred, expandable FAQ | Trust and answers sit next to the Ask-for-rate buttons (earlier commit on `product-reviews-rail-faq`) | see that branch |
| `tests/geo.test.mjs` (5 tests) | Keeps `llms.txt` and `site.ts` in step, checks Hindi guides keep the same table numbers and links as their English originals | `tests/geo.test.mjs` |

Left alone on purpose: no `Offer`, price, rating, review or speakable markup (prices are "ask for rate"; the 4.9 / 633
figures are shown on the page, not marked up); no new location pages; no AI-specific schema. Product rich results need an
offer, review or rating, so products are valid but not rich-result eligible. That is a deliberate trade for not
publishing prices.
